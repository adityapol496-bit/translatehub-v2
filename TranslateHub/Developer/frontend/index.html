    About this

    TranslateHub

    A multilingual real-time translation platform built to bridge language gaps for customer support and educational use cases — powered by a pre-trained translation model and automatic language detection.

    Real-time Translation

    Type or paste text and get an instant translation between 100+ languages, with automatic source-language detection.

    Voice Input

    Speak instead of typing — TranslateHub listens, transcribes, and translates your speech automatically.

    Conversation History

    Every translation you make is saved to your account, so you can revisit and reuse it anytime.

    Personal Profile

    Set your preferred language and photo once, and TranslateHub remembers it for you on every visit.

    100+ Languages Supported

    From English and Marathi to Japanese and Arabic — TranslateHub understands and translates over 100 languages in real time, so you can talk to anyone, anywhere.
